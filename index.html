Inbox Digest

A personal, read-only digest of one Gmail inbox, used only by its owner.

What it is

Inbox Digest is a small command-line tool that one individual runs on his own computer to keep track of his own Gmail inbox. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no account for anyone else, and it is not offered, sold or distributed to other people. Its only user is its owner, signing in with his own Google Account.

What it does

  • Runs a short list of saved Gmail searches chosen by the owner (for example: receipts, invoices, account notices).
  • For the matching messages, lists the sender, subject and date, so the owner sees what arrived without opening each one.
  • For receipts and invoices the owner selects, reads the message text or the attached PDF invoice and keeps only the lines that show an amount, a billing period or a renewal date, to build a personal record of what he has paid.

What it never does

  • It never sends, replies to, deletes, archives, labels or otherwise changes any email. Google access is read-only (the gmail.readonly permission).
  • It never shares, sells or publishes email data, and it never uses it for advertising.
  • It does not run on a server for other people; the digest stays on the owner's own computer.

How access works

The owner grants access once through Google's standard sign-in and consent screen. He can withdraw it at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Privacy

How Inbox Digest accesses, uses, stores and deletes Google user data is described in the Inbox Digest Privacy Policy.
